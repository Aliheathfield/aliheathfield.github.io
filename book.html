Start with what makes sense to you

Not Done Yet and the workbooks all draw on the same Living Life Well model, but none of them depends on the others. Read the book, start with a workbook, or both, in whichever order suits you.

The book

Not Done Yet

Life After 60: Ten Themes to Create What's Possible

You're standing at a crossroads. Perhaps you've just retired and feel unmoored without the structure that work provided. Maybe you've lost a partner and don't recognise the person looking back at you in the mirror. Whatever brought you here, this book shows you how to feel alive, matter, contribute, and design these years rather than simply endure them.
