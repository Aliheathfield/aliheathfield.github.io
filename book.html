Start with what makes sense to you

There is no single right way to use the Living Life Well books. You can begin with the main book, choose the workbook that fits where you are now, or start with the shorter reflective ebook. They are connected, but each serves a different purpose.

The main book

Not Done Yet

Life After 60: Ten Themes to Create What's Possible

Not Done Yet introduces the Living Life Well Model: ten areas of life that can help us understand where we are now, what matters, and what may need attention as we continue to age. It also introduces the three Living Life Well paths: Maintain & Prevent, Reinvent & Renew, and Health Navigator. The book is not about pretending we can control everything that happens. It is about recognising the circumstances we find ourselves in and making intentional choices about how we want to live within them.

A good place to start if: you want to understand the whole Living Life Well approach before deciding where to focus.

The practical workbooks

Each workbook takes one of the three Living Life Well paths and turns it into practical steps. They stand on their own, so you do not have to read Not Done Yet first.

Maintain & Prevent

Life After 60: Protecting What's Possible

For when life is mostly working and you want to keep it that way. This workbook helps you notice what is worth protecting, where small changes may be needed, and how regular check-ins can prevent gradual drift.

Choose this if: you want to maintain what is good, strengthen what matters and make small adjustments before problems become bigger.

Reinvent & Renew

Life After 60: Creating What's Possible

For when you feel ready for something different. This workbook helps you explore possibilities, try out new directions, rethink old assumptions and take practical steps towards a life that feels more like the one you want now.

Choose this if: you are curious about what else might be possible and want help turning ideas into action.

Health Changes

Life After 60: Navigating What's Possible

For people following the Health Navigator path — when health has changed the plan and you need practical ways to adapt while holding on to identity, connection, purpose and what still matters. This workbook helps you work with the circumstances you are in now, make thoughtful adjustments and keep sight of the parts of life that continue to matter.

Choose this if: health is influencing your choices more than it used to and you want practical ways to navigate what has changed.

A shorter, reflective read

It's Not Too Late

A collection of 47 short reflections on the Living Life Well themes

It's Not Too Late is different from the other books. It is a collection of 47 short reflections on the Living Life Well themes, written to be dipped into rather than worked through from beginning to end. Each reflection offers a thought, question or small step to consider.

Choose this if: you want something shorter and gentler that encourages you to pause, reflect and think about what might still be possible.
