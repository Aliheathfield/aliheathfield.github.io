Maintain and Prevent

Protecting what you love, and making the most of it.

You've built something worth keeping. This is where it continues to flourish.

What this path is for

This path is for you if you're mainly content and want to stay that way. It's about maintenance with intention: small tweaks, regular check-ins, and the kind of gentle attention that prevents drift while protecting joy. Minor changes and the odd adjustment really can make all the difference between a life that's just happening and one filled with intention, curiosity, and appreciation.

What you'll find here

A Theme Check-In to show you where attention will help
Monthly Check-Ins that keep you steady
Quick tune-up for ten life themes: work on one or work on all
Three starting points, depending on your energy and time
Freedom to adapt everything to your actual life
Minor tweaks, checks, and the odd change can and do make all the difference between a life that's just happening and one that's filled with intention, curiosity, and appreciation. Between ordinary and extraordinary.
