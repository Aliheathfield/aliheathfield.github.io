Live Your Way

Living Life Well

Intention, action and purpose — for life after 60.

Ali works with people navigating the real terrain of later life, whether facing significant change, managing health challenges, or simply wanting to live more fully and with greater intention.

Her own choice was this: let life happen and relinquish control to chance, or live with intention, enjoyment, meaning and purpose. She chose intention, and built the Living Life Well framework on that decision. It underpins everything she offers.

Find out more about Ali →

Wherever you are, there is a path from here.

  • Maintain and Prevent Life is largely working well and you want to keep it that way, with intention and care rather than by accident.
  • Reinvent and Renew You are navigating significant change or transition and want to find what comes next with clarity and purpose.
  • Health Navigator You are managing health challenges, whether your own or someone close to you, and need practical, grounded support.
