The main book

Not Done Yet

Life After 60: Ten Themes to Create What's Possible

You do not have to have everything worked out.

Life after 60 can bring freedom, but it can also bring changes in identity, routine, relationships, health, purpose and the way you see yourself. Not Done Yet offers a practical way to step back, look at your life as a whole, and decide what matters now.

It introduces the Living Life Well Model, a framework built around ten areas of life that can help you understand where you are, what may need attention, and what you want from what comes next.

The Living Life Well Model

A practical framework for life after 60

At the heart of Not Done Yet is the Living Life Well Model, created by Ali Heathfield. It explores ten areas that shape how we experience later life:

Self Narrative Meaning & Purpose Environment Health Finance Activities & Interests Relationships Community Planning

These areas do not sit separately. A change in one can affect several others. The Model is designed as a map, not a prescription. It helps you see the whole picture and decide where your attention may be most useful.

What you'll find inside
