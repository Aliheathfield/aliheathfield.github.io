Hello, I'm Ali

If life's changing shape, or already has, and you're working out what comes next, you're in the right place. If you're mainly content but feel that something's missing, or simply want to make sure it stays that way as you age, you're in the right place too.

Curious how this all began? Read Ali's story →

I'm an author, the creator of the Living Life Well Model, and a specialist in living well after 60.

I listen, sit with you, and work out what's really going on. Then I walk alongside you using the map I built for Living Life Well.

I weave together positive psychology, coaching, and life wisdom to find what's designed specifically for you at this particular stage of life, rather than a more generic approach.

I hold an MSc in Positive Psychology with Distinction from Buckinghamshire New University, and bring decades of coaching experience working with people through transition and growth.

Working beneath the surface

Trained deeply in how people think, communicate, and change, I hear what's beneath the surface as much as what's said, working with the whole person, not just the presenting question.

Real change often lives beneath what we say. I approach that deeper level with care, curiosity, and real respect for the person doing the work.

My style is warm, practical, and grounded in lived experience. What you see is what you get.
